This is a small SQLite-backed stock tracker whose public steps open an inventory database (creating the items table if needed), record a delivery against an existing product identified by its SKU, and list the products whose quantity is at or below their reorder level, lowest stock first. Its main routine is a hard-coded demonstration that opens a database file at a fixed location, adds 500 units to the product with SKU BOLT-10, and then prints one line per product that still needs reordering (SKU, name, quantity left and reorder level), or a note that nothing does. It needs console and filesystem permissions and modifies the database, but nothing in it adds new products, so the demonstration stops with an unknown-SKU error unless BOLT-10 was already stored by something else, which makes it a starting point rather than a finished tool.
