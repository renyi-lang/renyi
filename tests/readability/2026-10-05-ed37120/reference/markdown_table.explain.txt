Renders a header row and body rows of text as a Markdown table with aligned columns. The width of every column is the widest cell in it, the header included; the rendering is the header row, a divider row of dashes of each column's width, and the body rows, each cell padded on the right to its column's width, cells separated by " | " and the row framed by "| " and " |", rows joined by line breaks; a missing cell counts as empty. The entry point prints a three-column table of fruit, price and stock (apple 0.50 120; kiwi 0.80 7; watermelon 3.20 2). It needs the console and cannot fail.
