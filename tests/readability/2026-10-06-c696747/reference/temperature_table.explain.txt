Prints a Celsius-to-Fahrenheit conversion table: the header "Celsius  Fahrenheit", then one row for each Celsius value from -10 to 40 in steps of 10, with the Celsius value right-aligned in 7 characters, two spaces, and the Fahrenheit value right-aligned in 10 characters. The conversion is celsius times 9 over 5 plus 32 (0 gives 32, 100 gives 212, -40 gives -40). It needs the console and cannot fail.
