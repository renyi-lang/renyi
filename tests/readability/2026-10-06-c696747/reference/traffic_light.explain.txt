A traffic light as a state machine driven by timer and emergency events. The next light after an event: an emergency declared gives FlashingRed; an emergency cleared gives Red; a timer expiry cycles Red to Green, Green to Yellow, Yellow to Red, and leaves FlashingRed as it is. Running a sequence gives the light after each event, one entry per event. The entry point applies timer, timer, emergency declared, timer, emergency cleared from Red and prints one light per line: Green, Yellow, FlashingRed, FlashingRed, Red. It needs the console and cannot fail.
