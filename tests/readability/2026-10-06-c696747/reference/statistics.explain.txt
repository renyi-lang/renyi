Prints the mean, median and population standard deviation of the numbers given as command-line arguments. An argument that is not a number is skipped with the line "skipping <argument>: <error>". Each statistic prints as "<label>: <value rounded to three decimals>" ("mean", "median", "standard deviation"), or "<label>: no data" when no numbers were given; the three functions give nothing for an empty list and are public. It needs the console and the environment and cannot fail.
