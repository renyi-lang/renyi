Represents arithmetic expressions over decimals as a tree of numbers, additions, multiplications and negations; evaluates a tree to its value; and renders a tree in infix notation with parentheses around every addition and multiplication and a leading minus sign for a negation. The entry point builds (1 + 2) * -4 and prints "((1 + 2) * -4) evaluates to -12". It needs the console and cannot fail.
