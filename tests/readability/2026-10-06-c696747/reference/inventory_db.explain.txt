Keeps a small stock inventory in a SQLite database file and reports what needs reordering. Opening the inventory creates the items table (sku, name, quantity, reorder level) when it is missing. The low-stock query lists the items whose quantity is at or below their reorder level, lowest quantity first. Recording a delivery adds the amount to one item's quantity and fails with UnknownSku when no item has that sku. The entry point opens data/inventory.db, records a delivery of 500 units of BOLT-10, then prints each low-stock item as "<sku> <name>: <quantity> left, reorder at <level>", or "nothing to reorder" when there is none, and closes the connection. It needs the console and the filesystem. It fails with a database error or UnknownSku.
