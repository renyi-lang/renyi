A pure library (no entry point, no effects) that computes invoice totals exact to the cent. A line has a description, a quantity of at least 1 and a unit price of at least 0; an invoice has a customer, a list of lines and a discount percentage from 0 to 100. A line's total is quantity times unit price; the subtotal is the sum of the line totals; the total takes the percentage discount off the subtotal and rounds to two decimal places. Two tests check that a 10 percent discount on ten pens at 1.00 gives 9.00 and that an invoice without lines totals 0.
