Downloads three pages (example.com, example.org, example.net) in parallel, measures the length of each body in characters and prints one line per page, "<url>: <n> characters", from the largest to the smallest. It then downloads example.com and example.com/docs in parallel under a ten-second deadline and prints "home and docs together: <sum> characters". The reusable function takes a URL and returns the URL with its character count. It needs the console and HTTP network access. It fails with an HTTP error when a download fails and with a timeout when the deadline passes.
