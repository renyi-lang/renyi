Decides whether a set of roles grants everything an action requires. A permission is a name of the form "resource:action"; a role has a name and a set of permissions. The union of what several roles grant, the required permissions that none of the roles grants, whether the roles together grant every required permission, and the permissions two roles share are each a function. The entry point checks a viewer (posts:read) and an editor (posts:read, posts:write) against publishing (posts:read, posts:write, posts:publish): it prints "the editor may publish" when everything is granted, otherwise "missing: <permission>" for each missing permission in sorted order (here only posts:publish), and then "shared: <permissions>" with the permissions the two roles share (here posts:read). It needs the console and cannot fail.
