Describes and measures plane shapes: a circle with a radius greater than 0, a rectangle with a width and height greater than 0, or a point. The area is 3.14159 times the radius squared, width times height, or 0. The description is "a circle of radius <r>", "a <w> by <h> rectangle" or "a point", followed by " with area <area rounded to two decimals>". The largest of a list of shapes is the one with the greatest area, or nothing for an empty list. The entry point prints the descriptions of a circle of radius 1.5, a 2 by 3 rectangle and a point, then "largest: " followed by the description of the largest (the circle, area 7.07). It needs the console and cannot fail.
