Parses server log lines and counts the errors in each hour. A line has the form "timestamp LEVEL message": an ISO 8601 timestamp to the second, one of DEBUG, INFO, WARN or ERROR, and the rest of the line as the message. Parsing fails with Malformed (the line) when the line has fewer than three words or the first word is not a timestamp, and with UnknownLevel (the word) for any other level word. The hour of a timestamp is its first 13 characters (the date and the hour). The errors-per-hour function maps each hour to the number of ERROR entries in it. The entry point reads data/server.log, skips blank lines, parses every line (one bad line ends the program) and prints "<hour>: <n> errors" for each hour that has errors, earliest hour first. It needs the console and read access to the filesystem.
