Finds the tasks due soon and counts the working days left for each. A task line of the form "title, YYYY-MM-DD" is parsed into a task; it fails with MissingComma when the line does not split into exactly two parts and with an invalid-date error for a bad date. The working days between a start and a finish date are the Monday-to-Friday days after the start up to and including the finish (0 when they are equal; public holidays are not considered). The tasks due soon are those due between today and today plus a number of days, both inclusive, earliest first. The entry point takes today's date from the clock and three hard-coded tasks (quarterly report due 2026-10-09, renew certificate due 2026-11-30, team review due 2026-10-13) and prints each task due within ten days as "<title>: due <date>, <n> working days left"; it never uses the line parser. It needs the console and the clock.
