A pure library (no entry point, no effects) with the shipping cost and delivery rules of a web shop, called by the host application at checkout. A quote for a parcel (weight in grams greater than 0, destination Domestic, Europe or International, order total of at least 0) gives the cost, the delivery time in business days (2, 5 or 10 by destination) and whether shipping is free. A Domestic order with a total of at least 50.00 ships free at cost 0; otherwise the cost is the base price of the destination (4.90, 9.50 or 19.90) plus 2.50 per weight step, where the steps are the whole thousands in (grams minus 1), that is, every started kilogram beyond the first. Parcels decode from JSON and quotes encode to JSON.
