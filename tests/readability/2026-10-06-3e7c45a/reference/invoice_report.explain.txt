Prints a plain-text statement for invoices, reusing the invoice module for the totals. A statement line is the customer padded on the right to 12 characters, the number of lines padded on the left to 3, the text " lines", and the invoice total padded on the left to 10 (an empty ACME invoice renders as "ACME          0 lines      0.00"); the grand total is the sum of the invoice totals. The entry point builds two invoices (ACME: ten pens at 1.00 with a 10 percent discount, total 9.00; Globex: two notebooks at 4.50 and one stapler at 12.00 with no discount, total 21.00), prints their statement lines sorted by customer and then "grand total: 30.00". It needs the console and cannot fail.
