A pure library (no entry point, no effects) that parses, prints and compares semantic version numbers of the form major.minor.patch with non-negative integer parts and no pre-release or build suffix. Parsing fails with WrongPartCount (the input and the number of parts found) when there are not exactly three parts and with NotANumber (the input and the part) when a part is not a non-negative integer. A version prints as "1.4.2" and versions compare by major, then minor, then patch. A candidate satisfies a required version when the majors are equal and the candidate is not older. The latest of a list is its greatest version, or nothing for an empty list. Two tests cover printing and comparison.
