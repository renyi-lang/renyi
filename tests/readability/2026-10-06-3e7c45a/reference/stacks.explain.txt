A pure library (no entry point, no effects) with a generic last-in first-out stack and a bracket checker built on it. The stack operations are: an empty stack; push, which gives a copy with the item on top; pop, which gives the top item and the remaining stack, or nothing for an empty stack; peek, the top item without removing it; and a size ability with size and is-empty. The bracket checker says whether every round and square bracket in a text is closed by its partner in the right order, ignoring other characters. Two tests check popping an empty stack and that the last item pushed is on top.
